# STAR: Leadership

## Situation
In my junior year, I founded and led the university's "Cloud Native Computing Club" â€” a student organization focused on Kubernetes, containers, and cloud infrastructure. The club started with 3 members and no budget. Within one semester, we needed to establish credibility, secure funding, and deliver value to attract members.

## Task
Build a sustainable student organization that provides hands-on cloud infrastructure experience, secures university/industry sponsorship, and grows to 50+ active members.

## Action
1. **Defined a clear mission**: "Learn production-grade cloud infrastructure by running real services for campus clubs"
2. **Secured partnerships**: Approached CS department for $2k seed funding and AWS Educate for $5k credits â€” succeeded by proposing concrete projects (hosting club websites, CI/CD for student projects)
3. **Designed a project-based curriculum**: 8-week "Kubernetes from Zero" sprint with weekly labs, culminating in deploying a microservices app on EKS
4. **Created leadership pipeline**: Appointed 3 project leads (Infra, Education, Outreach) with clear ownership â€” I focused on strategy and external relations
5. **Built visibility**: Hosted 2 campus hackathons, published 5 technical blog posts, presented at regional student conference

## Result
- Grew to 67 members in 6 months (largest technical club in CS department)
- Secured $12k total funding (university + AWS + GitHub + Datadog)
- Deployed 12 real services for campus organizations (saving them ~$500/mo each)
- 15 members landed cloud/DevOps internships at FAANG and unicorns
- Club continues after my graduation with new leadership team

## Skills Demonstrated
- Organizational founding and strategy
- Sponsorship/fundraising
- Technical curriculum design
- Delegation and leadership development
- Community building
- Cross-functional collaboration (faculty, industry, students)

## Variations for Different Roles
- **Engineering Manager**: Emphasize hiring, mentoring, career development of team leads
- **DevRel/Technical PM**: Focus on curriculum design, community building, external partnerships
- **Startup Founder**: Highlight zero-to-one building, resource constraints, vision selling
